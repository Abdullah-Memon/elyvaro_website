A store built around discovery.

Modern Lifestyle & Discovery Store is Elyvaro's take on a place you come to look around, not just to buy. The idea is a store for modern living: everyday objects, small upgrades, and things you did not know you were looking for until you saw them.

Planned for Dubai, the store is being shaped around thirteen departments, each with its own visual world, under one unmistakable Elyvaro. The concept is still being worked out. Nothing is available to buy today.
