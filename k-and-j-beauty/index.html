Why Korean and Japanese beauty.

K and J takes its name from two traditions known for daily care first: gentle cleansing, consistent skincare, and colour that enhances rather than covers.

The range being prepared follows that logic. Basics you use every day, masks for the days you want more, and colour when you feel like it. For women and men, as one range.
